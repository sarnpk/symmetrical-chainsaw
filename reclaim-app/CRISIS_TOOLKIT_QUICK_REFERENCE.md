# Crisis Toolkit - Quick Reference Card

## 😰 ANXIETY
**Rule Out:** Am I unsafe, or am I uncomfortable?

**Skills:**
- 💧 Cold water on wrists (30s)
- 🫁 4-6 breathing (inhale 4, exhale 6)
- 👀 5-4-3 grounding (5 see, 4 hear, 3 feel)

**Affirmation:** "My anxiety is loud, not necessarily accurate. I am safe in this moment."

---

## 😔 DEPRESSION
**Rule Out:** Is this physical exhaustion, emotional exhaustion, or both?

**Skills:**
- 💧 Drink full glass of cold water
- ☀️ Face the sun for 2 minutes
- ✅ Complete one 3-minute task

**Affirmation:** "This is heavy. I'm doing what I can with what I have and that is more than enough."

---

## 😨 PTSD/FLASHBACK
**Rule Out:** Am I having a reaction to the past, or to a danger that is tangible in the present?

**Skills:**
- 🎨 Name 3 colors in the room
- 👣 Press feet firmly into floor
- 📅 Say: "Today is [date]. I am here."

**Affirmation:** "My body is remembering something painful. I can hold that feeling while remembering that now, it doesn't mean I'm unsafe."

---

## 😬 SOCIAL ANXIETY
**Rule Out:** Is this emotion a prediction, or an observable truth?

**Skills:**
- 🫁 Slow exhale to steady voice
- 👁️ Brief eye contact (1-2 seconds)
- 💬 Say: "How's your day going?"
- 🎭 Act opposite to fear, show up

**Affirmation:** "Feeling nervous doesn't make me awkward. It makes me human."

---

## 🔄 OCD/INTRUSIVE THOUGHTS
**Rule Out:** Is this an intrusive thought, or a risk someone I trust would tell me to worry about?

**Skills:**
- 🏷️ Label: "This is a sticky thought"
- ⏱️ Delay compulsion by 1 minute
- ↔️ Do the opposite of what OCD says

**Affirmation:** "My brain is sending a false alarm. I don't have to respond to it."

# Crisis Reframe - Custom Situation Feature

## 🎯 New Feature: Describe Your Own Situation

Users can now describe ANY situation in their own words, and the AI will:
1. **Analyze** what's happening
2. **Identify** narcissistic patterns
3. **Reframe** it in the user's favor
4. **Build** hope, control, and encouragement
5. **Turn** the negative into a positive turning point

---

## 💬 How It Works

### Step 1: User Selects "Describe Your Situation"
Instead of choosing a pre-defined crisis type, user clicks the custom option.

### Step 2: User Writes Their Story
They get a large text area to describe:
- What happened
- How they feel
- What they're struggling with
- What kind of reframe they need

### Step 3: AI Analyzes & Reframes
The AI:
- Reads their entire situation
- Identifies patterns and struggles
- Creates a personalized reframe
- References specific details they shared
- Turns it into hope and control

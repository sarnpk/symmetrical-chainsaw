## 🆕 Live Conversation Feature

### What Makes It Special:

**Two Input Fields:**
1. **Situation Context**: Describe what's happening
2. **Ongoing Conversation**: Paste your WhatsApp/text messages

**Flexible Name Input:**
- Put "I AM [name] AND SHE/HE IS [name]" in EITHER field
- Simulator checks both fields
- Works in situation context OR conversation
- Your choice!

**Example Usage:**

**Option A - Names in Situation:**
```
Situation Context:
I AM Syed naqvi AND SHE IS Begum New

She took my kids a week ago. We're discussing documents.

Conversation:
[11:59 AM] Syed naqvi: Message here
[11:59 AM] Begum New: Response here
```

**Option B - Names in Conversation:**
```
Situation Context:
She took my kids a week ago. We're discussing documents.

Conversation:
I AM Syed naqvi AND SHE IS Begum New

[11:59 AM] Syed naqvi: Message here
[11:59 AM] Begum New: Response here
```

**Both work perfectly!**

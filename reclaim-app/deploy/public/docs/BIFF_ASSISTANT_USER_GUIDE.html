What is BIFF?

BIFF stands for:

  • Brief - Keep it short (1-5 sentences max)
  • Informative - Facts only, no emotions or opinions
  • Friendly - Polite tone, like a neutral coworker
  • Firm - Clear boundaries, no wiggle room

Developed by Bill Eddy (High Conflict Institute), BIFF is specifically designed for dealing with high-conflict personalities, including narcissists, in co-parenting situations.

Why BIFF Works with Narcissists

🎭 Starves Drama

No emotional reaction = no narcissistic supply

🛡️ Prevents Gaslighting

Facts only = harder to twist reality

⚖️ Court-Ready

Professional tone = better evidence

🧱 Enforces Boundaries

Firm responses = less manipulation

Examples: Bad vs Good

Scenario: Late Pickup Accusation

❌ BAD (Not BIFF):

"I can't believe you're accusing me of being late! I'm ALWAYS on time. You're the one who's constantly late and making excuses. Remember last month when you were 45 minutes late and didn't even apologize? I'm so tired of your lies and manipulation. The kids deserve better than this constant drama."

Problems: Too long, emotional, defensive, brings up past, JADE

✅ GOOD (BIFF):

"Hi [Name], pickup is at 6 PM at the police station per the parenting plan. I'll have the kids ready. Thanks."

Why it works: Brief (2 sentences), factual, friendly tone, firm boundary

Scenario: Schedule Change Request

❌ BAD:

"I already have plans that day because I thought we were following the schedule we agreed on. I can't just drop everything whenever you decide you want to change things. This is exactly why we have a parenting plan - so we can both plan our lives."

✅ GOOD:

"Hi [Name], I'm unavailable that day. Let's stick to the current plan. Thanks."

Quick Reference: BIFF Checklist

Before sending, ask yourself:

  • ☑️ Is it under 50 words?
  • ☑️ Did I stick to facts only?
  • ☑️ Is my tone neutral/polite?
  • ☑️ Did I avoid JADE?
  • ☑️ Is my boundary clear?
  • ☑️ Would this look professional in court?

Communication Best Practices

📱 Use Documented Channels Only
  • Email or parenting apps (OurFamilyWizard, TalkingParents)
  • Creates paper trail for court
  • Avoid phone calls (no record)
  • Screenshot text messages immediately
⏰ Delay Responses
  • Wait 24-48 hours before replying to emotional messages
  • Respond only to logistics, ignore bait
  • One BIFF reply, then disengage
🎯 Stay Child-Focused
  • Only discuss kids' schedules, health, education
  • Ignore personal attacks
  • Don't discuss your personal life
  • Keep it business-like

When to Escalate

⚠️ Contact Your Attorney If:
  • Threats of violence or harm
  • Repeated violations of court orders
  • Parental alienation attempts
  • False accusations to authorities
  • Withholding children during your time

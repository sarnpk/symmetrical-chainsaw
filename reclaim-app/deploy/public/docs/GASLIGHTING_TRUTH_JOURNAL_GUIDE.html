🛡️ Gaslighting Truth Journal - Complete Guide

Gaslighting is when someone manipulates you into questioning your reality, memory, or sanity. This tool helps you document their lies and detect contradictions with AI.

🤖 AI-Powered Contradiction Detection

Our AI analyzes all your logged statements and automatically detects when they've contradicted themselves. It identifies 4 types:

  • Direct Opposite: "I never said that" vs proof they did
  • Timeline Conflict: Changing dates/times of events
  • Fact Denial: Denying objective reality
  • Blame Shift: Changing who was responsible

🎯 How to Use

Step 1: Log Immediately

Document gaslighting as soon as it happens. Fresh details = stronger evidence.

Step 2: Be Specific

Write exactly what they said (use quotes) and exactly what the truth is.

Step 3: Note Evidence

Mark if you have proof. Save screenshots, emails, texts immediately.

Step 4: Run AI Analysis

After logging 2+ statements, click "Run Analysis" to detect contradictions.

Step 5: Export Report

Generate professional HTML report for therapist or attorney.

🤖 AI Features

Contradiction Detection

AI compares all statements and finds where they've contradicted themselves:

  • Shows both conflicting statements side-by-side
  • Calculates confidence score (how certain the AI is)
  • Explains the contradiction in plain English
  • Shows days between contradictory statements

Credibility Scoring

AI generates a credibility score (0-100%) based on:

  • Number of contradictions
  • Frequency of gaslighting
  • Severity of lies
  • Evidence availability

Pattern Analysis

AI identifies which topics they gaslight about most:

  • Topic breakdown (finances, parenting, etc.)
  • Escalation warnings
  • Evidence strength assessment

⚠️ Red Flags

Severe Gaslighting Indicators

  • 🚩 Multiple contradictions per week
  • 🚩 Denying events you have proof of
  • 🚩 Rewriting history consistently
  • 🚩 Making you question your sanity
  • 🚩 Credibility score below 50%
  • 🚩 Gaslighting across multiple topics

If you see 3+ red flags: This is severe psychological abuse. Share your report with a therapist.

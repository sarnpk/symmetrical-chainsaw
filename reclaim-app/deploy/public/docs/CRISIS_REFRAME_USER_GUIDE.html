🚨 Crisis Reframe - User Guide

What is Crisis Reframe?

Crisis Reframe is an AI-powered emergency tool designed to help you regain control during panic situations caused by narcissistic abuse. It provides immediate emotional stabilization through powerful reframing, expert psychological techniques, and hope-building narratives.

🎯 Use Crisis Reframe when:
  • You just experienced a discard or breakup
  • The narcissist exploded in rage at you
  • You're being given the silent treatment
  • They're trying to hoover you back
  • You feel the urge to text/call them
  • You're spiraling into panic
  • You need immediate grounding

How to Use Crisis Reframe

Step 1: Select Your Crisis Type

Choose what just happened from 8 options:

  • 💔 Discard/Breakup - They suddenly ended it
  • 🔥 Narcissistic Rage - They exploded at you
  • 🤐 Silent Treatment - They're ignoring you
  • 💌 Hoovering - They're trying to come back
  • 😢 Devaluation - They're treating you terribly
  • 🎭 Gaslighting - They're denying reality
  • 🔄 Cycle Repeat - It's happening again
  • 🆘 Describe Your Situation - Tell AI in your own words
💡 Pro Tip: If your situation doesn't fit the categories, choose "Describe Your Situation" and tell the AI exactly what happened. The more details you share, the more personalized your reframe will be.

Step 2: Answer Quick Questions

The AI will ask 2-3 quick questions to personalize your reframe:

  • How long were you together?
  • Is this the first time?
  • How do you feel right now?

These are optional but help create a more personalized response.

Step 3: Confirm "I'm In Control"

Before generating your reframe, you'll click a button that says "I'm in control". This physical action helps restore your sense of agency.

Step 4: Read Your Personalized Reframe

The AI generates a structured reframe with 8 sections:

  1. ✋ YOU ARE IN CONTROL RIGHT NOW - Immediate control restoration
  2. 🫂 What You're Feeling Is Real - Validation of your pain
  3. 🎯 What This Actually Is - Pattern recognition
  4. 💡 The Reframe - Meaning change with powerful metaphors
  5. 🌅 Your Future - Hope narrative (6 months from now)
  6. 🛡️ Right Now, You Need To... - Immediate actions
  7. 🌟 How To Pass Through This Hard Time - Coping strategies
  8. 💪 Your Power In This Moment - Final empowerment

Step 5: Complete the Control Checklist

Check off commitments you'll make:

  • I will not text/call them for the next hour
  • I will tell one person how I'm feeling
  • I will write down what happened
  • I will revisit this reframe if panic returns
  • I will be kind to myself today

Step 6: Track Your "Survived" Time

Watch the counter track how long you've survived without reacting. Every minute is a victory!

Understanding the Reframe Sections

🔥 Powerful Metaphors

The AI uses vivid metaphors to challenge your thinking:

  • "Would you stay in a car that's about to crash?" - Challenges staying as dangerous
  • "This is a prison break" - Reframes discard as liberation
  • "Would you drink from a poisoned well?" - Challenges reconciliation urges
  • "You're the driver now" - Restores control

🧠 Expert Psychological Techniques

The coping strategies section includes evidence-based techniques:

  • STOP Technique - Stop, Take breath, Observe, Proceed
  • 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding - Sensory awareness
  • 24-Hour Rule - Impulse control
  • Unsent Letter - Emotional release
  • Box Breathing - 4-4-4-4 pattern

🌟 Positive Activities

Specific things you can do right now:

  • Physical: Jumping jacks, walking, exercise
  • Creative: Writing, drawing, painting
  • Social: Call a friend, join support group
  • Self-care: Bath, cooking, reorganizing
  • Distraction: Comedy series, learning new skills

Tips for Maximum Benefit

✅ DO:
  • Read slowly - don't rush through it
  • Complete the control checklist
  • Try at least one coping strategy
  • Revisit when panic returns
  • Save or print for future reference
  • Share with your therapist if helpful
⚠️ DON'T:
  • Skip the "I'm in control" confirmation
  • Read while driving or operating machinery
  • Use as a replacement for emergency services
  • Share your reframe publicly (it's personal)
  • Ignore the immediate actions section

Custom Situation Feature

The "Describe Your Situation" option lets you tell the AI exactly what happened in your own words.

How to Use It:

  1. Select "Describe Your Situation"
  2. Write as much detail as you want (the more, the better)
  3. Optionally tell the AI what kind of reframe you need
  4. Describe how you're feeling

Example:

"My ex just texted me after 2 weeks of silence saying they miss me and made a mistake. I'm so confused and want to respond but I know I shouldn't. I feel like I'm going crazy."

The AI will analyze your situation and provide a personalized reframe that:

  • Addresses your specific circumstances
  • References details you shared
  • Provides relevant coping strategies
  • Turns your situation into hope and control

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take to generate a reframe?

A: 2-3 seconds. The AI works quickly because we know you're in crisis.

Q: Can I save my reframes?

A: Yes! All reframes are automatically saved to your history. You can also print or save them manually.

Q: What if I need to use it again for the same crisis?

A: You can revisit your saved reframes anytime from the history section. This doesn't count against your monthly limit.

Q: Is this a replacement for therapy?

A: No. Crisis Reframe is peer support, not therapy. It's designed for immediate crisis intervention, not long-term treatment.

Q: What if I'm having suicidal thoughts?

A: Please call emergency services immediately:

  • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233

Q: Can I share my reframe with my therapist?

A: Absolutely! Use the print/save feature to share it with your therapist or support system.

Emergency Resources

🚨 If you're in immediate danger:
  • Call 911 (Emergency Services)
  • 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • 1-800-799-7233 (National Domestic Violence Hotline)
  • Text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line)

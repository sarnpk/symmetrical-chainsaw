🎭 Role Reframing User Guide

Reclaim Your Identity Beyond Their Narrative

What is Role Reframing?

Role Reframing is the process of identifying and rejecting the false roles a narcissist assigned to you, then reclaiming your authentic identity.

Narcissists cast you in roles that serve their narrative: the villain, the savior, the incompetent one, the crazy one. These roles are not who you are - they're characters in their distorted story.

Common Narcissist-Assigned Roles

🦹 The Villain

"You're the reason everything goes wrong"

🤡 The Crazy One

"You're too sensitive, overreacting, unstable"

🛟 The Savior

"You must fix me, save me, sacrifice for me"

👶 The Incompetent One

"You can't do anything right without me"

🎪 The Entertainment

"Your pain and reactions amuse me"

🏆 The Trophy

"You exist to make me look good"

How Role Assignment Works

  1. Projection: They project their flaws onto you
  2. Gaslighting: They make you doubt your reality
  3. Repetition: They repeat the role until you believe it
  4. Isolation: They cut you off from people who see the truth
  5. Punishment: They punish you for stepping out of the role

Role Reframing Process

Step 1: Identify the Assigned Role

Ask yourself:

  • What role did they constantly cast me in?
  • What labels did they repeatedly use?
  • How did they describe me to others?
  • What behaviors did they expect from me?

Step 2: Recognize It's False

❌ Their Narrative:

"You're too emotional and unstable. You can't handle anything. You need me to function."

Reality Check:

  • Did people before them see you this way?
  • Do people outside the relationship see you this way?
  • Were you this way before meeting them?
  • Does this align with your actual behavior and values?

Step 3: Reframe to Your Truth

✅ Your Truth:

"I am emotionally intelligent and responsive. I handled their abuse while maintaining my responsibilities. I am capable, strong, and independent."

Reframing Examples

Role: "The Crazy One"

Their Version: "You're paranoid, jealous, and unstable. You imagine things that aren't real."
Your Reframe: "I had normal reactions to abnormal behavior. My intuition was correct. I'm not crazy - I was being gaslit."

Role: "The Villain"

Their Version: "You ruined everything. You're selfish and cruel. You destroyed our family."
Your Reframe: "I set boundaries to protect myself. I chose my wellbeing. I'm not the villain - I'm the survivor."

Role: "The Incompetent One"

Their Version: "You can't do anything right. You need me to handle everything. You'd fall apart without me."
Your Reframe: "I am capable and competent. They undermined me to maintain control. I'm thriving without them."

Reclaiming Your Identity

Who Are You Really?

Strip away their labels and rediscover yourself:

  • 🎨 What are your actual strengths?
  • 💝 What do you value?
  • 🌟 What brings you joy?
  • 🎯 What are your goals?
  • 🤝 How do healthy people describe you?
  • 💪 What have you accomplished despite them?

Dealing with Flying Monkeys

They may have convinced others of your assigned role.

Your response:

  • You don't owe anyone explanations
  • People who believe lies without asking you aren't your people
  • Your truth will reveal itself through your actions
  • Focus on people who see the real you

Affirmations for Role Reframing

  • ✨ I reject the roles they assigned to me
  • ✨ I define who I am, not them
  • ✨ Their narrative is not my reality
  • ✨ I am reclaiming my authentic self
  • ✨ I trust my own perception of myself
  • ✨ I am not their character - I am my own person
  • ✨ My identity is mine to create

Warning Signs You're Still in the Role

  • You automatically defend yourself when accused
  • You feel guilty for normal boundaries
  • You question your own competence constantly
  • You seek validation from people who knew them
  • You still try to prove you're not what they said
💡 The Goal: You no longer care what they think or say about you. Their opinion is irrelevant to your identity.

Remember

You are not their story. You are the author of your own narrative.

Their roles were costumes. You can take them off and burn them.

Your authentic self was always there. It was just buried under their projections.

Reframing is liberation. You're not who they said you were. You're who you choose to be.

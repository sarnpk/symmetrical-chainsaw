🎯 What Is Belief Reframe?

Belief Reframe is a CBT-based system designed to identify, challenge, and replace false beliefs installed by narcissistic abuse. It helps you systematically rebuild your self-worth and reconnect with reality through evidence-based therapeutic techniques.

🧠 The Science: Narcissistic abuse installs false beliefs about your worth, capabilities, and reality. CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) techniques help identify these distorted thoughts and replace them with balanced, evidence-based beliefs.

🔍 Core Components

💭False Beliefs Tracking

Document and monitor harmful beliefs installed by abuse:

  • Track belief strength (1-10 scale)
  • Record when/how beliefs were installed
  • Monitor progress over time
  • Identify patterns and triggers

🛡️Counter-Evidence Collection

Build evidence that contradicts false beliefs:

  • Document achievements and successes
  • Record positive feedback from others
  • Note moments of competence and strength
  • Collect objective proof of your worth

📚Origin Memories

Understand how false beliefs were installed:

  • Document specific incidents of abuse
  • Identify manipulation tactics used
  • Connect current beliefs to past trauma
  • Process memories in a safe context

🚀 Getting Started

1 Identify Your First False Belief

Start with a belief that feels particularly strong or limiting:

  • "I'm not good enough"
  • "I can't trust my own judgment"
  • "I'm responsible for others' emotions"
  • "I don't deserve love/respect"
2 Rate the Belief Strength

On a scale of 1-10, how strongly do you believe this right now? Be honest - this is your baseline for tracking progress.

3 Add Origin Memories

Document when/how this belief was installed:

  • Specific incidents or patterns
  • What was said or done
  • How it made you feel
  • The manipulation tactic used
4 Start Collecting Counter-Evidence

Look for any evidence that contradicts the false belief:

  • Past achievements
  • Positive feedback
  • Times you succeeded
  • Moments of strength
5 Use AI Reality Testing

Let the AI coach guide you through challenging the belief with CBT techniques. This is where the real transformation happens.

💡 Best Practices

🎯 Focus on One Belief at a Time

Don't overwhelm yourself. Work deeply on one false belief before moving to the next. Quality over quantity.

📊 Track Your Progress

Update belief strength regularly. Seeing the numbers decrease over time provides powerful motivation and validation.

🔄 Daily Counter-Evidence

Make it a habit to notice and record evidence that contradicts your false beliefs. Small moments add up to big changes.

🤖 Use AI Tools Regularly

The AI coach is most effective with regular use. Schedule weekly reality testing sessions for consistent progress.

⚠️ Important Notes

🩺 Professional Support

Belief Reframe is a therapeutic tool but not a replacement for professional therapy. If you're experiencing severe symptoms, please seek professional help.

⏰ Be Patient

Changing deeply ingrained beliefs takes time. Some beliefs may have been reinforced for years or decades. Progress is often gradual but real.

🔒 Privacy & Safety

All your data is encrypted and private. However, be mindful of your emotional state when working with traumatic memories. Take breaks as needed.

🎉 Success Indicators

You'll know Belief Reframe is working when you notice:

  • Decreased belief strength scores over time
  • Automatic questioning of negative self-talk
  • Increased self-compassion and patience
  • Better emotional regulation during triggers
  • Growing collection of counter-evidence
  • Feeling more grounded in reality
  • Increased confidence in your perceptions

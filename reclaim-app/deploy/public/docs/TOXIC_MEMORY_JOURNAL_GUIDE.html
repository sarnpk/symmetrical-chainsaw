📸 Toxic Memory Journal Guide

What is Toxic Memory Journal?

A quick and short way to capture toxic/abusive incidents as they happen or shortly after. Think of it as "evidence snapshots" - brief notes with optional media proof.

Why Use This?

  • Quick capture: Jot down what happened in a few sentences
  • Add evidence: Attach audio, video, or images as proof
  • AI analysis: Identify manipulation tactics (gaslighting, DARVO, etc.)
  • Pattern tracking: See recurring abuse patterns over time
  • Legal/therapeutic use: Timestamped evidence for professionals

How to Use

  1. Click "Add Memory" to document an incident
  2. Describe what happened - Keep it brief and factual
  3. Add evidence (optional): Upload audio recordings, videos, or photos
  4. Add tags (optional): Categorize by type (gaslighting, manipulation, verbal abuse, etc.)
  5. Save: Your memory is timestamped and stored securely
Example Entry:
"She yelled at me for 2 hours because I forgot to text back immediately. Called me selfish and said I don't care about her feelings. Made me apologize repeatedly."

AI Analysis Feature

After saving a memory, you can click "AI Analyze Memory" to get:

  • NPD Tactics Identified: Gaslighting, projection, blame-shifting, etc.
  • Validation: Confirmation that your experience was real and harmful
  • Suggested Beliefs: Toxic beliefs this incident may have installed in you

Evidence Types

  • Audio: Voice recordings of arguments or verbal abuse
  • Video: Visual evidence of incidents
  • Images: Screenshots of texts, photos of damage, etc.
⚠️ Safety First: Only record evidence if it's safe to do so. Your safety is more important than documentation.

Tips for Effective Documentation

  • Be factual: Describe what happened without interpretation
  • Include context: What triggered it? Where were you?
  • Note your feelings: How did it make you feel?
  • Document immediately: Capture details while fresh
  • Use tags: Makes it easier to find patterns later

Difference from Main Journal

Toxic Memory Journal: Quick snapshots with evidence - "What happened"
Main Journal: Deeper reflection and processing - "How I feel about it"

Privacy & Security

All memories are:

  • Encrypted and stored securely
  • Only accessible by you
  • Protected by Row Level Security (RLS)
  • Can be exported for legal/therapeutic use

When to Use This

  • After a toxic incident occurs
  • When you have evidence to preserve
  • To track patterns of abuse
  • For legal documentation
  • To validate your experiences
💡 Remember: This is separate from your regular journal. Use it for quick incident documentation with evidence, not for deep emotional processing.

# Session Management - Quick User Guide

## ✅ YES! Session Management is NOW Available

All your practice sessions are automatically saved and you can now manage them!

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do

### 1. **View Session History**
Click the "Session History" button (top right) to see all your past practice sessions.

### 2. **Load Old Sessions**
Click "Load" on any session to:
- Review past conversations
- Continue practicing from where you left off
- See your previous responses and feedback

### 3. **Delete Sessions**
Click "Delete" on any session to permanently remove it.

---

## 📋 How to Use

### View Your History
```
1. Open Narcissist Simulator
2. Click "Session History" button (top right)
3. See all your past sessions
```

### Load a Session
```
1. Click "Session History"
2. Find the session you want
3. Click "Load" button
4. Session opens with full conversation
5. Continue or just review
```

### Delete a Session
```
1. Click "Session History"
2. Find session to delete
3. Click "Delete" button
4. Confirm deletion
5. Session removed
```

## 📊 What You'll See

### Session Card Shows:
- **Status Badge**: Active, Completed, or Abandoned
- **Type**: Overt, Covert, or Malignant narcissist
- **Scenario**: Custody, Text, Email, Boundary, or Custom
- **Message Count**: Total messages in conversation
- **Date & Time**: When session started
- **Preview**: First message of conversation

## 💡 Tips

### When to Load a Session:
- Review how you handled a situation
- Continue an unfinished practice
- Show progress to your therapist
- Learn from past responses

### When to Delete a Session:
- Remove test sessions
- Clean up old practice
- Delete sessions you don't need
- Keep history organized

### Best Practices:
- Keep important sessions for reference
- Review old sessions to see improvement
- Delete test/practice sessions regularly
- Use custom context for real situations

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Are sessions saved automatically?**
A: Yes! Every session is saved automatically as you practice.

**Q: Can I continue an old session?**
A: Yes! Click "Load" and you can continue from where you left off.

**Q: What happens if I delete a session?**
A: It's permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

**Q: How many sessions can I save?**
A: Unlimited! The history shows your 20 most recent sessions.

**Q: Can others see my sessions?**
A: No! Your sessions are completely private.

**Q: Do I need to do anything to save sessions?**
A: No! They save automatically as you practice.

# Narcissist Traits Detection Guide

## Core Traits & Detection Patterns

### 1. GASLIGHTING

**Definition**: Making someone question their reality, memory, or sanity

**Language Markers**:
- "That never happened"
- "You're crazy/insane/delusional"
- "You're too sensitive"
- "You're overreacting"
- "That's not what I said"
- "You're misremembering"
- "Nobody would believe you"
- "You're making things up"

**Patterns to Detect**:
- Denial of events that occurred
- Contradicting previous statements
- Dismissing victim's feelings as invalid
- Rewriting history
- Claiming victim is mentally unstable

**Confidence Scoring**:
- Direct denial phrases: +30%
- Reality distortion: +25%
- Sanity questioning: +20%
- Dismissal of feelings: +15%
- Contradiction of facts: +10%

**Example**:
```
Narcissist: "I never said that. You're making it up."
Victim: "But you literally just said it 5 minutes ago."
Narcissist: "You're crazy. I would never say something like that."
```

### 2. LOVE-BOMBING

**Definition**: Excessive praise, attention, and promises early in relationship

**Language Markers**:
- "You're perfect"
- "I've never felt this way before"
- "You complete me"
- "You're my soulmate"
- "I can't live without you"
- "You're the only one who understands me"
- "We're meant to be together"
- "I want to spend the rest of my life with you"
- Excessive compliments
- Rapid commitment talk

**Patterns to Detect**:
- Superlatives (best, perfect, amazing)
- Future promises (marriage, forever, etc.)
- Intensity and speed of declarations
- Excessive compliments
- Constant contact/attention
- Idealization language

**Confidence Scoring**:
- Superlatives: +20%
- Future promises: +25%
- Intensity markers: +20%
- Constant contact references: +15%
- Idealization language: +20%

**Example**:
```
Narcissist: "You're the most amazing person I've ever met. 
I can't imagine my life without you. Let's get married next month. 
You're perfect in every way."
```

---

### 3. HOOVERING

**Definition**: Sudden contact after silence, attempting to suck victim back in

**Language Markers**:
- "I miss you"
- "I've changed"
- "I've been thinking about us"
- "Can we talk?"
- "I made a mistake"
- "I want to try again"
- "I've been miserable without you"
- "Nobody compares to you"
- "I realize now what I did wrong"
- Sudden nostalgia references

**Patterns to Detect**:
- Sudden contact after silence
- Claims of change
- Nostalgia/reminiscing
- Apologies (often insincere)
- Requests to reconnect
- Emotional appeals
- Timing after victim has moved on

**Confidence Scoring**:
- Sudden contact after silence: +30%
- Change claims: +20%
- Nostalgia: +15%
- Apologies: +15%
- Reconnection requests: +20%

**Example**:
```
[After 2 months of no contact]
Narcissist: "I miss you so much. I've been thinking about 
our good times. I've really changed. Can we please talk? 
I made a huge mistake letting you go."
```

### 4. TRIANGULATION

**Definition**: Bringing a third party into conflict to make victim jealous or feel excluded

**Language Markers**:
- "My ex was better at..."
- "Everyone agrees with me"
- "My new partner thinks you're..."
- "My friends all say..."
- "My therapist says you're..."
- "My mom thinks..."
- Comparisons to others
- References to other people's opinions
- "Everyone knows..."

**Patterns to Detect**:
- Third-party references
- Comparisons to others
- Using others' opinions as weapons
- Exclusion language
- Jealousy-inducing references
- Authority figures backing them up

**Confidence Scoring**:
- Third-party references: +25%
- Comparisons: +20%
- Opinion weaponization: +25%
- Exclusion language: +15%
- Authority backing: +15%

**Example**:
```
Narcissist: "My ex was so much better at this than you. 
Everyone at work thinks you're being unreasonable. 
My therapist agrees with me, not you."
```

### 5. PROJECTION

**Language Markers**:

- "You're the abuser"

- Blame-shifting
- "You're just like..."

**Patterns to Detect**:
- Accusation reversal
- Blame-shifting

- Defensive accusations
- Character attacks
- Turning tables

**Confidence Scoring**:
- Direct accusation reversal: +30%

- Defensive accusations: +15%
- Turning tables: +10%

**Example**:
```

Narcissist: "No, YOU'RE the manipulative one. You're trying 
to make me look bad. You're the abuser here, not me."
```

## Implementation Notes

1. **Phrase Matching**: Use exact phrase matching + fuzzy matching for variations
2. **Context**: Consider surrounding text for better accuracy
3. **Negation**: Handle negations ("I'm NOT..." should reduce confidence)
4. **Intensity**: Weight ALL CAPS and multiple punctuation marks higher
5. **Patterns**: Look for combinations of traits, not just individual ones
6. **False Positives**: Be conservative with confidence scores
7. **Disclaimers**: Always include that this is educational, not diagnostic

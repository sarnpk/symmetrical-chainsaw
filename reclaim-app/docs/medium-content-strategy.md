## Optimization Tips

### Before Publishing Each Article:
1. **Add a compelling cover image** (use Canva or Unsplash)
   - Person looking at phone (crisis/detector articles)
   - Peaceful scene (grey rock/health check)
   - Courtroom or documents (gaslighting journal)

2. **Submit to Publications:**
   - "Better Humans" (self-improvement)
   - "The Startup" (tech + mental health)
   - "P.S. I Love You" (relationships)
   - "Mental Health Matters"
   - "The Ascent" (personal growth)

3. **Optimize First Paragraph:**
   - Hook must be in first 2 sentences
   - Make it shareable (people will quote it)
   - Create curiosity gap

4. **Add Internal Links:**
   - Link between your articles
   - Creates "binge reading" effect
   - Keeps readers on your content

### After Publishing:
1. **Share on Social Media** (within 1 hour)
   - Twitter: Pull quote + link
   - LinkedIn: Professional angle
   - Reddit: r/NarcissisticAbuse, r/raisedbynarcissists (be genuine, not spammy)
   - Facebook groups: Narcissistic abuse support groups

2. **Engage in Comments** (first 24 hours critical)
   - Respond to every comment
   - Ask follow-up questions
   - Share additional resources
   - Be vulnerable and authentic

3. **Update with User Stories** (after 1 week)
   - Add "Update" section with anonymized success stories
   - Shows social proof
   - Boosts engagement

## What Makes These Articles Work

### Authentic Voice:
- Written in first person
- Specific details (2:47 AM, 8 months, Tuesday)
- Emotional honesty
- Admits mistakes and doubts
- Conversational tone

### Story Structure:
- Hook (crisis moment)
- Problem (can't see the pattern)
- Discovery (found the tool)
- Transformation (what changed)
- Proof (specific results)
- Call to action (try it yourself)

### Anti-AI Signals:
- Contractions (I'm, don't, can't)
- Sentence fragments for emphasis
- Rhetorical questions
- Personal anecdotes
- Imperfect grammar (how humans actually write)
- Emotional language
- Self-doubt and vulnerability

### SEO Keywords Naturally Included:
- Narcissistic abuse
- Gaslighting
- Emotional abuse
- Toxic relationships
- Grey Rock
- Co-parenting
- Mental health
- Recovery
- Boundaries
- Manipulation

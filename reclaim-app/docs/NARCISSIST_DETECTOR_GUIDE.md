## Understanding the Results

### Narcissist Types

**Overt (Grandiose)**
- Openly arrogant and superior
- Demands attention and admiration
- Brags about achievements
- Dismissive of others
- Aggressive when challenged

**Covert (Vulnerable)**
- Plays victim constantly
- Passive-aggressive behavior
- Guilt-trips and martyrdom
- Appears sensitive but manipulative
- Indirect hostility

**Malignant**
- Cruel and vindictive
- Enjoys causing pain
- Threatening and intimidating
- Paranoid and controlling
- Sadistic tendencies

### Confidence Scores

- **80-100%**: Very likely this type
- **60-79%**: Probably this type
- **40-59%**: Possible, but uncertain
- **Below 40%**: Unlikely this type

### Manipulation Tactics

**Gaslighting**
- Denying things they said/did
- Making you question your memory
- Twisting facts
- "That never happened"

**Love Bombing**
- Excessive praise and attention
- Then sudden coldness
- Creates emotional dependency
- Keeps you off-balance

**DARVO**
- Deny the abuse
- Attack you for bringing it up
- Reverse Victim and Offender
- Make themselves the victim

**Triangulation**
- Bringing third parties into conflicts
- Comparing you to others
- Creating jealousy
- Dividing relationships

**Hoovering**
- Attempts to suck you back in
- After discard or no contact
- Promises to change
- Love bombing returns

### Severity Score (1-10)

- **1-3**: Mild narcissistic traits
- **4-6**: Moderate narcissistic behavior
- **7-8**: Severe narcissistic patterns
- **9-10**: Extreme/dangerous behavior

## Response Strategies

### Grey Rock Method

**What it is:** Becoming boring and unresponsive

**How to do it:**
- Keep responses brief
- Share no emotional information
- Be as interesting as a grey rock
- Don't react to provocations

**Example:**
- Them: "You're so selfish!"
- You: "Okay."

### BIFF Method

**What it is:** Brief, Informative, Friendly, Firm

**How to do it:**
- **Brief**: Keep it short
- **Informative**: Stick to facts
- **Friendly**: Neutral tone
- **Firm**: Clear boundaries

**Example:**
- Them: "You're always changing the schedule!"
- You: "The schedule hasn't changed. Pickup is still at 5pm on Friday."

### No Contact

**What it is:** Completely cutting off communication

**When to use:**
- After breakup/divorce
- When safety is at risk
- When co-parenting isn't required
- When all other methods fail

### Modified Contact

**What it is:** Minimal necessary communication only

**When to use:**
- Co-parenting situations
- Shared business/property
- Can't do full no contact

**How to do it:**
- Only discuss necessary topics
- Use written communication
- Keep it factual
- Set clear boundaries

## Common Questions

**Q: Is this a medical diagnosis?**
A: No. This is an educational tool. Only qualified professionals can diagnose NPD.

**Q: Should I show them the results?**
A: No. This will likely escalate conflict and give them ammunition.

**Q: Can narcissists change?**
A: Rarely. True change requires them to acknowledge the problem and commit to intensive therapy, which most won't do.

**Q: Why do I need to know their type?**
A: Understanding their patterns helps you protect yourself and respond effectively.

**Q: What if the analysis is wrong?**
A: The AI provides probabilities, not certainties. Trust your own experiences and consult professionals.

**Q: Can I use this for legal purposes?**
A: No. Consult with attorneys and mental health professionals for legal matters.

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
A: The AI is trained on narcissistic behavior patterns but is not perfect. Use it as one tool among many.

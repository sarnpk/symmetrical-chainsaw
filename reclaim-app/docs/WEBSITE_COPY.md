**Q: Is my data really private?**
A: Yes. Your data is encrypted with AES-256 — the same standard banks use. We can't read it. We don't sell it. We don't use it to train AI models. It's yours, encrypted, and protected.

**Q: Can the person I'm documenting find out I'm using Reclaim?**
A: No. Reclaim has no visible branding, no push notifications with sensitive content, and can be accessed from any device. Your usage is completely invisible.

**Q: What if they find my account?**
A: You can set a decoy PIN. If someone enters the wrong PIN, they see a fake dashboard with generic wellness content. Your real data stays hidden.

#### Evidence & Legal Use

**Q: Can I use this in court?**
A: Yes. Every entry is timestamped, cryptographically signed, and stored with GPS location. Reclaim exports are formatted for court admissibility and have been accepted in family court, divorce proceedings, and workplace harassment cases.

A: Absolutely. Reclaim generates court-ready PDF exports with timestamps, metadata, and pattern analysis that attorneys can use directly. We also provide attorney referral resources.

**Q: Do I need to create an account?**
A: No. Our free assessment tools work instantly — no signup, no email, no tracking. When you're ready for the full experience, creating an account takes 30 seconds.

**Q: What if I'm still in the relationship?**

#### Pricing

**Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?**

## Copy Guidelines for Implementation

### Headline Formula
`[Emotional truth] + [What Reclaim does] + [Outcome]`
Example: "Your reality is not up for debate. Reclaim helps you document it, prove it, and move forward."

### CTA Formula
`[Action verb] + [Specific benefit] + [Low friction]`
Example: "Start free assessment" (not "Get Started" or "Sign Up")

### Writing Rules
1. **Lead with the pain, not the product.** "You've been told you're too sensitive" before "Reclaim validates your experience."
2. **Use "you" language.** Never "users" or "customers." Always "you."
3. **Be specific.** "AES-256 encrypted" beats "bank-level security." "80,000+ assessments" beats "thousands of users."
4. **Address objections before they arise.** "No signup required" appears before they wonder if they need to create an account.
5. **Never minimize.** Don't say "minor abuse" or "small things." If they're here, it matters.
6. **End every section with a CTA.** Never let a section trail off. Always point to the next action.

### Tone Spectrum
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Hero / Emotional sections | Warm, validating, intimate |
| Feature descriptions | Clear, benefit-focused, confident |
| Pricing | Transparent, low-pressure, empowering |
| Legal / Evidence | Authoritative, specific, credible |
| Crisis / Safety | Direct, urgent, compassionate |
| FAQ | Thorough, reassuring, practical |

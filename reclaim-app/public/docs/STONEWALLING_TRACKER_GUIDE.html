💡 Real-World Examples

Example 1: Silent Treatment After Boundary

Situation: You told your partner you need one evening per week for self-care.

Their Response: Complete silence for 48 hours. Won't make eye contact. Leaves room when you enter.

What to Log:

  • Type: Silent Treatment
  • Duration: 2,880 minutes (48 hours)
  • Trigger: "Set boundary about needing personal time"
  • Emotional State: 8/10 → 2/10
  • Impact Level: 9/10
  • What You Needed: "Acknowledgment that my needs matter"

Example 2: Topic Avoidance Pattern

Situation: Every time you bring up co-parenting schedule, they change subject or walk away.

Pattern Over 3 Weeks:

  • Week 1: Asked about summer schedule → Left to "take a call"
  • Week 2: Mentioned kids' therapy → Started scrolling phone
  • Week 3: Brought up school expenses → "I don't want to talk about this"

AI Insight: "100% avoidance rate on child-related topics. Suggests control tactic to avoid accountability."

Example 3: Emotional Unavailability

Situation: You share that your parent is sick and you're worried.

Their Response: Blank stare. "Okay." Returns to watching TV.

What to Log:

  • Type: Emotional Unavailability
  • Duration: Ongoing (no reconnection)
  • Trigger: "Shared vulnerable feelings about family"
  • What You Needed: "Comfort, empathy, or even just acknowledgment"
  • What Happened: "Complete emotional shutdown"

⚠️ Red Flags to Watch

Escalation Warning Signs

  • 🚩 Duration increasing over time
  • 🚩 Happening more frequently (weekly → daily)
  • 🚩 Expanding to more topics
  • 🚩 Physical withdrawal becoming more extreme
  • 🚩 Your emotional state consistently dropping below 3/10
  • 🚩 Zero successful reconnections

If you see 3+ red flags: Consider this a pattern of emotional abuse. Consult with a therapist or domestic violence advocate.

🎓 Understanding Stonewalling

Why narcissists stonewall:

  • Control tactic - forces you to chase them
  • Punishment for asserting yourself
  • Avoids accountability
  • Creates anxiety and self-doubt
  • Maintains power imbalance

Impact on you:

  • Emotional exhaustion
  • Self-blame ("What did I do wrong?")
  • Walking on eggshells
  • Suppressing your needs
  • Chronic anxiety

💡 Remember

Stonewalling is emotional abuse. You deserve to be heard. Your needs matter. This tool helps you see the pattern clearly and respond from a place of strength, not desperation.

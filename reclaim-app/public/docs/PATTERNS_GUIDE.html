📊 Pattern Analysis Guide

What is Pattern Analysis?

Pattern Analysis helps you identify recurring behaviors, timing patterns, and trends in your documented experiences. By analyzing your journal entries over time, you can:

  • Recognize behavioral patterns and triggers
  • Understand when experiences are most likely to occur
  • Track frequency and severity trends
  • Get AI-powered insights about your situation
  • Receive a risk assessment based on documented patterns

How to Use Pattern Analysis

1. Select Your Timeframe

Choose the period you want to analyze:

  • Last Month: Recent patterns and immediate trends
  • Last 3 Months: Short-term behavioral patterns
  • Last 6 Months: Medium-term trends (recommended)
  • Last Year: Long-term patterns and cycles

2. View Automatic Charts

Charts load automatically when you select a timeframe:

📅 Experience Frequency

Monthly breakdown showing when experiences occurred most frequently

📊 Behavior Types

Distribution of different abuse types documented in your entries

🕐 Time Patterns

Day-of-week analysis showing when experiences typically happen

3. Run Full Analysis

Click the "Analyze" button to generate:

  • Risk Indicator: AI assessment of pattern severity (Low/Moderate/High)
  • AI Insights: Personalized analysis of your documented patterns
  • Evidence References: Links to specific journal entries supporting the assessment
💡 Tip: Run analysis after documenting several experiences to get meaningful insights. The more data you have, the more accurate the patterns.

Understanding the Risk Indicator

Risk Levels

  • 🟢 Low Risk: Isolated experiences or insufficient evidence of patterns
  • 🟡 Moderate Risk: Some concerning patterns detected
  • 🔴 High Risk: Multiple concerning patterns indicating potential danger

What the Risk Score Means

The risk score (0-20) is calculated based on:

  • Frequency of documented experiences
  • Types of behaviors documented
  • Escalation patterns over time
  • Safety ratings from your entries
  • Specific red-flag indicators
⚠️ Important: The risk indicator is informational only and not a professional diagnosis. If you feel unsafe, contact emergency services immediately (911) or the National Domestic Violence Hotline (1-800-799-7233).

AI Insights Explained

After running analysis, you'll receive AI-generated insights that may include:

  • Pattern Recognition: Recurring behaviors or cycles identified
  • Trigger Analysis: Common situations or times when experiences occur
  • Escalation Trends: Whether severity is increasing, decreasing, or stable
  • Safety Recommendations: Personalized suggestions based on your patterns
  • Documentation Gaps: Areas where more detail could strengthen your records
💡 Pro Tip: Review AI insights regularly to stay aware of emerging patterns and adjust your safety planning accordingly.

Best Practices

For Accurate Pattern Analysis:

  1. Document Consistently: Regular entries provide better pattern detection
  2. Be Detailed: Include context, behaviors, and your emotional state
  3. Use Safety Ratings: Rate each experience's severity (1-5 scale)
  4. Tag Abuse Types: Categorize behaviors for better analysis
  5. Review Regularly: Run analysis monthly to track changes

When to Run Analysis:

  • After documenting 5+ experiences (minimum for meaningful patterns)
  • Monthly to track trends over time
  • Before important decisions (leaving, legal action, etc.)
  • When preparing documentation for professionals
  • If you notice changes in behavior patterns

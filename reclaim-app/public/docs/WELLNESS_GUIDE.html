💚 Wellness Dashboard - User Guide

What is the Wellness Dashboard?

Your daily space for healing, growth, and self-care. This is where you check in with yourself, track your emotional state, build coping strategies, and access tools that support your recovery journey.

Mood Check-In

Track your emotional state daily to identify patterns and triggers.

How to Use:

  • Rate your mood: 1 (very low) to 10 (excellent)
  • Select emotions: Choose from anxious, sad, angry, hopeful, calm, etc.
  • Add notes: What happened today? What triggered this mood?
  • Track patterns: Review past check-ins to see trends
Example Check-In:
Mood: 4/10
Emotions: Anxious, Sad, Confused
Notes: "He texted me after 2 weeks of silence. Felt the old pull to respond. Didn't. Proud but shaky."

Why This Matters:

  • Validation: Your feelings are real and deserve to be acknowledged
  • Pattern recognition: See what days/situations affect you most
  • Progress tracking: Notice when good days become more frequent
  • Therapy support: Share mood data with your therapist

Coping Strategies

Build your personal toolkit of strategies that help you through difficult moments.

How to Use:

  • Browse strategies: See recommended coping techniques
  • Add your own: What works for YOU? Add it to your library
  • Rate effectiveness: Track which strategies help most
  • Quick access: When you're struggling, open this and pick one
Example Strategies:
• Grounding: 5-4-3-2-1 technique (5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you touch, 2 you smell, 1 you taste)
• Movement: 10-minute walk, yoga, dancing to one song
• Connection: Text a safe friend, call your therapist, pet your dog
• Distraction: Watch a comfort show, read, do a puzzle
• Release: Journal, cry, scream into a pillow, punch a punching bag

Building Your Library:

The best coping strategies are the ones that work for YOUR nervous system. Some people need movement, others need stillness. Some need connection, others need solitude. Experiment and track what helps.

⚠️ Healthy vs Unhealthy Coping:
Healthy: Exercise, journaling, talking to friends, therapy, meditation, creative outlets
Unhealthy: Excessive alcohol/drugs, self-harm, isolating for days, stalking their social media, revenge fantasies

If you're using unhealthy coping mechanisms, please reach out to a therapist or crisis line. No judgment - just support.

Tips for Daily Wellness Practice

  • Check in daily: Even if it's just 2 minutes, consistency matters
  • Be honest: This is for YOU. No one else sees it. Be real about how you feel
  • Track patterns: Review your mood history weekly to spot triggers
  • Build your toolkit: Add coping strategies as you discover what works
  • Celebrate small wins: Got out of bed? That's a win. Didn't respond to their text? That's a win.
  • Use affirmations: Read them when you're doubting yourself
  • Don't judge yourself: Bad days happen. Healing isn't linear.

When to Seek Additional Help

This dashboard is a tool, not a replacement for professional help. Please reach out to a therapist if:

  • Your mood is consistently low (3 or below) for more than 2 weeks
  • You're having thoughts of self-harm or suicide
  • You're using unhealthy coping mechanisms regularly
  • You're unable to function in daily life
  • You're in an actively dangerous situation
💡 Finding a Therapist:
Look for therapists who specialize in:
• Narcissistic abuse recovery
• Trauma (PTSD, C-PTSD)
• Domestic violence
• EMDR or somatic therapy

Resources: Psychology Today, BetterHelp, RAINN, local domestic violence organizations

You are doing better than you think. The fact that you're here, using these tools, checking in with yourself - that's courage. That's growth. Keep going.

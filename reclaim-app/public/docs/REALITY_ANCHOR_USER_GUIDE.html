🎯 What Is Reality Anchor?

Reality Anchor is a daily emotional detachment routine designed to keep you grounded in reality and protected from narcissistic manipulation. It consists of three evidence-based practices that anchor you to your authentic self.

🧠 The Science: Narcissistic abuse creates emotional dysregulation and reality distortion. Daily anchoring practices help rebuild your emotional boundaries and strengthen your connection to objective reality.

🌅 The Three Daily Anchors

🌄Morning Intentions 5-10 min

When: First thing in the morning, before checking phone/messages

Purpose: Set your emotional boundaries for the day and anchor to your values

  • Set 3 intentions for how you want to feel today
  • Choose your emotional boundaries
  • Affirm your worth independent of others' opinions
  • Visualize yourself responding calmly to triggers

⏸️Mental Pause 2-3 min

When: Midday or when feeling triggered/overwhelmed

Purpose: Reset your nervous system and reconnect with reality

  • Take 10 deep breaths
  • Ground yourself: 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you feel
  • Ask: "What is actually happening vs. what am I assuming?"
  • Remind yourself of your morning intentions

🌙Decompression Ritual 10-15 min

When: End of day, before bed

Purpose: Process the day objectively and release emotional residue

  • Journal 3 objective facts about your day
  • Identify any manipulation attempts you noticed
  • Celebrate moments you stayed anchored to reality
  • Set intentions for tomorrow's emotional boundaries

Narcissist Detector

Learn how to use the Narcissist Detector to analyze text for narcissistic patterns

What is the Narcissist Detector?

The Narcissist Detector analyzes messages, conversations, and text to identify patterns commonly associated with narcissistic behavior. It uses AI to evaluate language patterns and provide insights.

Important: This tool is for educational and self-awareness purposes only. It does not diagnose anyone. Only qualified professionals can diagnose personality disorders.

How to Use

  1. Open the tool: Navigate to Narcissist Detector from the sidebar
  2. Paste text: Enter the message, email, or conversation you want to analyze
  3. Click Analyze: The AI will process the text and identify patterns
  4. Review results: Read through the identified traits and explanations
  5. Save or export: Keep a record for your documentation

What It Detects

Gaslighting Language

Phrases that make you question your reality, memory, or perception of events.

Love Bombing

Excessive flattery or affection used to manipulate or create dependency.

Blame Shifting

Turning situations around to make you feel responsible for their behavior.

Triangulation

Involving third parties to create jealousy, insecurity, or control.

Silent Treatment

Withholding communication as punishment or manipulation.

Future Faking

Making promises about the future to keep you engaged without intent to follow through.

Examples

Red Flag Language

"You're crazy, that never happened. You always make things up. Nobody else would put up with you."

This contains gaslighting, blame shifting, and devaluation.

Healthy Language

"I understand you feel that way. Let's talk about what happened and find a solution together."

This shows empathy, accountability, and willingness to communicate.

Tips

Tip: Analyze multiple messages over time to identify recurring patterns rather than judging based on a single message.
Tip: Save your analyses to build a documentation record that can be useful for therapy or legal purposes.
Warning: No AI tool can diagnose a personality disorder. Use this as one data point among many, not as a definitive judgment.

What to Do After

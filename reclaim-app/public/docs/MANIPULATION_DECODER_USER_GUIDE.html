🎯 What Is Manipulation Decoder?

Manipulation Decoder is an AI-powered tool that analyzes text messages, emails, voicemails, and conversations to identify manipulation tactics used by narcissists and abusers. It provides both pattern matching and advanced AI analysis to help you recognize gaslighting, emotional manipulation, and other toxic communication patterns.

🧠 The Science: Narcissists use predictable manipulation tactics. By identifying these patterns, you can protect yourself from emotional manipulation and respond more effectively to toxic communication.

🛡️ Grey Rock Response System

What is Grey Rock?

Grey Rock is a technique where you become as boring and unresponsive as a grey rock, giving the manipulator no emotional reaction to feed on.

AI-Generated Responses:

  • Contextually appropriate for the specific manipulation
  • Emotionally neutral and non-reactive
  • Click-to-copy for easy use
  • Multiple options to choose from

Example Grey Rock Responses:

  • "I understand your perspective."
  • "I'll consider that."
  • "Noted."
  • "I need time to think about this."
  • "That's your opinion."

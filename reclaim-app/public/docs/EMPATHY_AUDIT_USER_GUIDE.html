💔 Empathy Audit User Guide

Measure the Empathy Imbalance in Your Relationship

What is an Empathy Audit?

An Empathy Audit is a tool to objectively measure who gives empathy and who receives it in a relationship.

In healthy relationships, empathy flows both ways. In narcissistic relationships, empathy is a one-way street - you give endlessly, they take constantly.

Why Empathy Audits Matter

  • 📊 Provides objective data on relationship imbalance
  • 🔍 Reveals patterns you might minimize or excuse
  • 💡 Validates your experience with concrete evidence
  • ⚖️ Helps you see the relationship clearly
  • 🛡️ Protects against gaslighting ("I'm always there for you!")
  • 📈 Tracks changes over time

The 3 Empathy Audit Tools

The app has 3 tabs to help you measure and redirect your empathy:

Tab 1: Situation Audits

What it does: Tracks specific incidents where you needed support and evaluates their response.

How to use it:

  1. Click "Situation Audits" tab
  2. Select situation type (sick, bad day, loss, stressed, good news)
  3. Describe what happened
  4. Check boxes for their response (8 questions)
  5. App calculates empathy score (0-4)

Example: Situation Audit

Situation: "I was sick"
Description: "I had the flu with 102° fever. Could barely get out of bed."

Their Response:
  • ❌ Did NOT ask how I was feeling
  • ❌ Did NOT listen without interrupting
  • ❌ Did NOT validate my emotions
  • ❌ Did NOT offer comfort
  • ✅ Made it about themselves ("Who's going to take care of ME?")
  • ✅ Minimized my experience ("It's just a cold")
  • ✅ Blamed me ("You always get sick")
  • ❌ Did NOT get angry

Empathy Score: -3/4 (0 positive, 3 negative)

💡 Pro Tip: Do a situation audit every time you need support. After 5-10 audits, you'll see the pattern clearly. Most narcissists score 0 or negative consistently.

Tab 2: Distribution Analysis

What it does: Shows where your emotional energy is going overall.

How to use it:

  1. Click "Distribution Analysis" tab
  2. Adjust sliders to show % of empathy you give to:
    • Ex-Partner
    • Children
    • Self
    • Others (friends, family)
  3. Must total 100%
  4. Write reflection on what surprised you
  5. Click "Get AI Guidance" for personalized advice

Example: Distribution Analysis

Current Distribution (Unhealthy):
  • Ex-Partner: 50% 😰
  • Children: 20%
  • Self: 10%
  • Others: 20%

Reflection: "I'm shocked that half my emotional energy still goes to her. I'm constantly worried about her reactions, trying to keep her calm, managing her emotions. Meanwhile I'm exhausted and my kids get the leftovers."

Goal Distribution (Healthy):
  • Ex-Partner: 5% ✅ (logistics only)
  • Children: 50% ✅
  • Self: 25% ✅
  • Others: 20% ✅

Why this works: Kids get the most, you get self-compassion, ex gets minimal (just enough for co-parenting logistics).

Tab 3: Guilt Support

What it does: Addresses the guilt you feel about detaching emotionally.

How to use it:

  1. Click "Guilt Support" tab
  2. Type what you feel guilty about
  3. Use voice input for longer reflections
  4. Read guilt-busting affirmations
  5. Get AI support for your specific guilt

Example: Guilt Support

Guilt Feeling:

"I feel guilty that I don't care about her problems anymore. She texts me about her bad day and I feel... nothing. I used to drop everything to help her. Now I just think 'not my problem.' Am I becoming cold and heartless?"

Guilt-Buster Response:

You're not cold—you're protecting yourself. Here's why your guilt is misplaced:

  • ✅ She's an adult responsible for her own emotions
  • ✅ You gave empathy for years—she weaponized it
  • ✅ Your children need you emotionally available
  • ✅ Detachment is survival, not cruelty
  • ✅ You're not her therapist, parent, or emotional support animal

Real-World Examples

Scenario: You Lost Your Job

❌ Narcissist Response:

"Great, now how are we going to pay bills? This is so stressful for ME. You should have worked harder. I can't deal with this right now - I have my own problems."

Empathy Score: 0/10 - Made it about them, blamed you, no support

✅ Healthy Response:

"I'm so sorry, that must be really hard. How are you feeling? We'll figure this out together. What do you need from me right now?"

Empathy Score: 10/10 - Validated feelings, offered support, asked about needs

Scenario: You're Sick with the Flu

❌ Narcissist Response:

"You're always sick. I never get to be sick. Who's going to take care of ME? You're so dramatic about a little cold."

✅ Healthy Response:

"Oh no, you poor thing. Let me get you some medicine and soup. Rest, I'll handle everything today."

Scenario: You Got a Promotion

❌ Narcissist Response:

"That's nice. I could have gotten that promotion if I wanted. Don't let it go to your head. You'll probably mess it up anyway."

✅ Healthy Response:

"That's amazing! I'm so proud of you! You worked so hard for this. Let's celebrate!"

Red Flags in Empathy Patterns

⚠️ Warning Signs of Empathy Imbalance:
  • You can't remember the last time they comforted you
  • You hesitate to share problems because of their reaction
  • They compete with your pain ("I have it worse")
  • They punish you for having needs
  • You always end up comforting them about your problem
  • They accuse you of being "needy" or "dramatic"
  • You feel guilty for needing support
  • They only show empathy when others are watching

How to Use All 3 Tools Together

Weekly Empathy Audit Routine:

  1. Monday: Do a Situation Audit for any support moment from the weekend
  2. Wednesday: Update your Distribution Analysis—are you still giving too much to ex?
  3. Friday: Process guilt in Guilt Support tab—what are you feeling guilty about this week?
  4. Monthly: Review all situation audits—look for patterns

The Empathy Scorecard (From Situation Audits)

After 10 situation audits, you'll see the pattern:

Situation Their Score Pattern
You were sick -2/4 Made it about themselves
You had bad day 0/4 Minimized experience
You shared good news -1/4 Competed/minimized
You were stressed -3/4 Blamed you, got angry

Average Score: -1.5/4 — This is empathy bankruptcy. You're giving to someone who can't reciprocate.

What to Do with Audit Results

If the Imbalance is Severe:

  • 📝 Document the pattern (use this tool regularly)
  • 🛡️ Stop expecting empathy from them
  • 🤝 Seek support from empathetic people
  • ⚖️ Consider if this relationship is sustainable
  • 💪 Validate your own feelings

Confronting Them (Usually Backfires):

If you show them the audit results, expect:

  • "You're keeping score? That's toxic!"
  • "I'm ALWAYS there for you! You're ungrateful!"
  • "You're too needy. Normal people don't need this much."
  • Gaslighting about specific incidents
  • Temporary love-bombing to "prove" they care

The audit is for YOU, not them. It's to validate your reality, not to change them.

Healing from Empathy Depletion

You've been giving empathy without receiving it. You're depleted.

Refill Your Empathy Tank:

  • 💝 Give yourself the empathy you gave them
  • 🤗 Surround yourself with empathetic people
  • 🧘 Practice self-compassion
  • 📖 Journal about your unmet needs
  • 💬 Therapy with a trauma-informed therapist
  • 🚫 Stop giving empathy to people who don't reciprocate

Remember

You are not asking for too much. Empathy is a basic human need in relationships.

You are not too sensitive. You're responding normally to empathy deprivation.

You deserve reciprocal empathy. One-way empathy is exploitation, not love.

The audit doesn't lie. Trust the pattern, not their words.

## Chat Design Options

**Option 1: Open Direct Messaging**
- Any community member can message any other member
- Like Twitter/Reddit DMs
- Pros: Easy connection, community building
- Cons: Potential for harassment, spam

**Option 2: Restricted Messaging**
- Only message people you've interacted with (liked/commented on their posts)
- Or mutual "connection" system
- Pros: Safer, reduces spam
- Cons: Less spontaneous connection

**Option 3: Group-Based Only**
- Chat only within support groups (like your mockGroups)
- Group admins moderate
- Pros: Structured, easier moderation
- Cons: No private 1-on-1 support

**Option 4: Hybrid (Recommended)**
- Support group chats (moderated by group leaders)
- Direct messaging only after mutual interaction (both users commented/liked each other's content)
- Pros: Balance of safety and connection
- Cons: More complex

## Moderation Considerations

**For Direct Messages:**
- Report button in every conversation
- Auto-flag messages with abusive keywords
- Moderators can view reported conversations
- Block user feature
- Message limits (e.g., 5 new conversations per day)

**For Group Chats:**
- Group moderators can remove messages
- Kick/ban users from groups
- Approval required to join private groups

# New Features Quick Reference Guide
## For Trauma Survivors Healing from Narcissistic Abuse

---

## 🎯 THE 6 PRIORITY FEATURES

### 1. **REALITY ANCHOR ROUTINE** ⭐ FLAGSHIP
**What it does:** Builds emotional detachment through daily practices
**Why it matters:** Directly implements the research's core therapeutic approach

#### Four Components:
- **Morning Intention** (5 min): Set daily intention for peace & children's well-being
- **Reality Log** (2 min): Document incidents factually, identify NPD traits
- **Mental Pause** (10 sec): Prepare before any interaction with her
- **Decompression Ritual** (15-30 min): Discharge emotional static after contact

**Who needs it:** Everyone (especially those struggling with emotional detachment)
**Subscription:** Foundation (limited), Recovery (full), Empowered (advanced)

---

### 2. **NPD TRAIT LIBRARY & EDUCATION**
**What it does:** Helps you recognize and name manipulation tactics
**Why it matters:** "Objectifies her behavior" - turns confusion into data points

#### Key Features:
- Comprehensive trait definitions (victim-playing, gaslighting, triangulation, etc.)
- Real-world examples
- Trigger identification for each trait
- Response strategies
- Searchable database

**Who needs it:** Anyone trying to understand what's happening
**Subscription:** Foundation (read-only), Recovery+ (with annotations)

---

### 3. **MANIPULATION DECODER**
**What it does:** Real-time analysis of messages/conversations
**Why it matters:** Provides objective reality check when you're confused

#### Key Features:
- Paste a message → Identify manipulation tactics
- Analyze past conversations
- Pattern matching ("This is similar to...")
- Emotional impact assessment
- Reality check: "Is this your fault?"

**Who needs it:** Anyone receiving confusing messages
**Subscription:** Recovery (5/day), Empowered (unlimited)

---

### 4. **EXPECTATION MANAGEMENT & MINDSET SHIFT**
**What it does:** Helps you accept NPD as permanent and reframe your role
**Why it matters:** "Your pain comes from expecting a healthy person to emerge"

#### Three Modules:
- **Diagnosis Acceptance:** Understand NPD is permanent, the person you married wasn't real
- **Role Reframing:** Shift from "spouse" to "project manager for difficult co-parenting"
- **Empathy Redirection:** Channel empathy toward children and yourself

**Who needs it:** Anyone still hoping for change or feeling guilty
**Subscription:** Recovery+

---

### 5. **CO-PARENTING STABILITY TRACKER**
**What it does:** Monitors your emotional stability for children's benefit
**Why it matters:** "Your emotional detachment is the foundation of your children's future safety"

#### Key Features:
- Daily stability check-in (1-10 scale)
- Mood tracking linked to co-parenting interactions
- "Sanctuary home" assessment
- Children's emotional safety checklist
- Parenting wins tracker
- Stress management tools

**Who needs it:** Co-parents with children
**Subscription:** Recovery+

---

### 6. **BEHAVIOR PATTERN DASHBOARD & RECOVERY METRICS**
**What it does:** Visualizes manipulation cycles and your progress
**Why it matters:** Quantifies healing and provides objective evidence of patterns

#### Key Features:
- Timeline of incidents with NPD traits
- Escalation pattern graphs
- Trigger identification
- Your response patterns (emotional vs. logical)
- Progress metrics: Emotional detachment level
- Predictive alerts: "Based on patterns, watch for..."
- Recovery progress: Emotional stability, boundary strength, self-compassion growth

**Who needs it:** Anyone wanting to see their progress
**Subscription:** Empowered

## ✨ KEY TAKEAWAY

The **Reality Anchor Routine** is your daily shield against manipulation and confusion. By practicing these four simple steps each day, you're:

1. **Protecting your peace** (Morning Intention)
2. **Documenting reality** (Reality Log)
3. **Preparing for interactions** (Mental Pause)
4. **Recovering from contact** (Decompression Ritual)

This is not about building hatred. It's about building a clear, factual boundary between your reality and her disorder.

**You can do this. Your children need you to do this.**

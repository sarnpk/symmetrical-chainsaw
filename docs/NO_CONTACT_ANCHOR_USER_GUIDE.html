🎯 What Is No Contact Anchor?

No Contact Anchor is a powerful tool designed to help you stay strong during withdrawal from narcissistic relationships. It treats no-contact like addiction recovery—because that's exactly what it is.

⚠️ Important: Breaking a trauma bond is physiological, not just psychological. You will experience withdrawal symptoms. This tool helps you ride them out.

🚀 Getting Started (5 Minutes)

1 Set Your No-Contact Start Date

Navigate to No Contact Anchor and set the date you went no-contact. This starts your streak counter.

2 Document "Why I Left"

Your toxic memories automatically populate here. Add more specific abuse incidents to strengthen your anchor.

  • Be specific: "He raged at me for 2 hours" not "He was mean"
  • Include dates and emotional impact
  • Tag incident types (gaslighting, rage, silent treatment)
3 Review Your Safety Plan

Make sure your emergency contacts are up-to-date in your Safety Plan. You'll need them during crisis moments.

🆘 Crisis Mode: "Before You Contact Them"

When You Feel the Urge to Contact:

  1. Click the big red button - "Before You Contact Them"
  2. Read your "Why I Left" list - Remember the reality, not the fantasy
  3. See your streak - Don't break your progress
  4. Call someone safe - Use your Safety Plan contacts
  5. Log the urge - Track your progress

🧠 Understanding Withdrawal

What you're feeling is normal:

  • Anxiety & Panic: Your nervous system is recalibrating
  • Intense Longing: This is the "drug" talking, not reality
  • Only Remembering Good Times: Your brain is trying to get its "fix"

The Truth: These feelings will pass. Every contact resets your healing. You cannot wean yourself off a drug by taking small doses.

🧪 The Science Behind It

Why This Works:

Trauma Bonding is Biochemical:

  • Your brain releases dopamine during "good" moments with the abuser
  • The unpredictability creates an addiction cycle
  • Withdrawal symptoms are real and physiological
  • No contact allows your nervous system to heal

Evidence-Based Approach:

  • Cognitive Anchoring: "Why I Left" list counters selective memory
  • Behavioral Tracking: Urge logs identify patterns
  • Visual Progress: Graphs provide motivation during weak moments
  • Social Support: Safe contacts break isolation

❓ Common Questions

Q: What if I break no contact?

A: Don't beat yourself up. Reset your start date and begin again. Every attempt teaches you something. The tool is here to support you, not judge you.

Q: How long until the urges stop?

A: Everyone is different, but most people see significant reduction by 30-60 days. Your urge graph will show your personal timeline.

Q: What if I don't have toxic memories documented?

A: Start documenting them now. Go to Toxic Memories and add incidents. The more specific your "Why I Left" list, the stronger your anchor.

Q: Can I use this for low contact instead of no contact?

A: The tool is designed for no contact because that's what breaks the trauma bond. Low contact keeps you in the addiction cycle. However, if no contact isn't possible (shared custody), use it to track contact-free days.

🎓 Key Principles to Remember

The Non-Negotiables:

  1. No Contact is Not Negotiable: You cannot heal while still taking doses of the drug
  2. Withdrawal is Temporary: The pain you feel now will pass
  3. Your Memory is Unreliable: Trust your documented evidence, not your feelings
  4. Every Contact Resets Progress: One text can undo weeks of healing
  5. You Deserve Peace: The chaos was not your fault, but healing is your responsibility

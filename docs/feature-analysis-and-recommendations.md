# Reclaim App: Feature Analysis & Recommendations
## Based on Trauma-Informed Research for Narcissistic Abuse Survivors

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The research text emphasizes **emotional detachment, reality anchoring, and mindset shifts** for trauma survivors dealing with narcissistic personality disorder (NPD). The current Reclaim app has strong foundational features but lacks specific tools aligned with the research's core therapeutic practices.

---

## CRITICAL GAPS & NEW FEATURE RECOMMENDATIONS

### 🎯 PRIORITY 1: REALITY ANCHOR ROUTINE (Core Feature)

**Research Alignment:** "This routine is not about building hatred; it's about building a clear, factual boundary between your reality and her disorder."

**Recommended Implementation:**

#### 1.1 Morning Intention Module
- **Purpose:** Prime the brain for emotional detachment
- **Features:**
  - Daily intention prompt (5 minutes)
  - Pre-written affirmations specific to co-parenting with NPD
  - Custom affirmation builder
  - Streak tracking (motivation)
  - Optional notification reminders
  
**Example Affirmations:**
- "My only goal today is my peace and my children's well-being"
- "I release the need to manage her emotions or expect normalcy"
- "Her actions are a reflection of her disorder, not my worth"
- "I am a project manager for a difficult co-parenting project"

#### 1.2 Reality Log (Enhanced Journal)
- **Purpose:** Objective documentation to counter emotional manipulation
- **Features:**
  - Structured template: Date → Event → Factual Description → NPD Trait Match
  - Emotion-free writing prompts
  - NPD trait library (covert narcissism, victim-playing, gaslighting, etc.)
  - Quick-tag system for pattern recognition
  - Private, password-protected entries
  - "Doubt Buster" feature: Re-read past logs when tempted to excuse behavior
  
**Template Structure:**
```
Date: [XX/XX]
Event: [Brief description]
Factual Details: [What happened, not how it felt]
NPD Trait Identified: [Victim-playing, Gaslighting, Triangulation, etc.]
Pattern Consistency: [Is this consistent with past behavior?]
```

#### 1.3 Mental Pause Pre-Interaction Tool
- **Purpose:** Break automatic empathetic response before contact
- **Features:**
  - Pre-interaction checklist (10 seconds)
  - Armor visualization guide
  - Grey Rock mantra reminder
  - Breathing exercise (4-7-8 technique)
  - Interaction type selector (text, call, pickup/dropoff)
  - Quick-access coping strategy cards

#### 1.4 Decompression Ritual Post-Interaction
- **Purpose:** Discharge emotional static after contact
- **Features:**
  - Physical options: Timer for walk/exercise, push-up counter
  - Creative options: Playlist integration, journaling prompt
  - Mindfulness options: Guided breathing (5-minute meditation)
  - Emotion release: Write & tear (digital version)
  - Ritual completion tracker
  - Mood check-in before/after

### 🎯 PRIORITY 2: EXPECTATION MANAGEMENT & MINDSET SHIFT

**Research Alignment:** "Your pain comes from expecting a healthy person to emerge. You must kill that expectation, for your own survival."

**Recommended Implementation:**

#### 2.1 Diagnosis Acceptance Module
- **Purpose:** Reframe NPD as permanent, not changeable
- **Features:**
  - Educational content on NPD permanence
  - "The Mask" visualization: Understanding the person you married wasn't real
  - Grief processing guide (mourning the illusion)
  - Acceptance affirmations
  - Interactive exercises: "What I expected vs. What is real"
  - Trigger identification: When do you slip back into hope?

#### 2.2 Role Reframing Tool
- **Purpose:** Shift from "spouse" to "project manager"
- **Features:**
  - Role definition guide
  - Co-parenting logistics tracker
  - Boundary matrix: What's your responsibility vs. hers?
  - Decision-making framework (emotional vs. logical)
  - "Difficult counterpart" scenario builder
  - Expectation reset checklist

#### 2.3 Empathy Redirection Module
- **Purpose:** Channel empathy toward children and self
- **Features:**
  - Empathy audit: Where is your empathy going?
  - Self-compassion exercises
  - Children-focused empathy practices
  - "Why I'm detaching for my children" reflection
  - Guilt-busting affirmations
  - Progress tracking: Emotional stability improvements

---

### 🎯 PRIORITY 3: NPD TRAIT LIBRARY & EDUCATION

**Research Alignment:** "Accept the 'Diagnosis' as Permanent"

**Recommended Implementation:**

#### 3.1 NPD Trait Encyclopedia
- **Purpose:** Help users recognize and name manipulation tactics
- **Features:**
  - Comprehensive NPD trait definitions
  - Covert vs. Overt narcissism comparison
  - Real-world examples (anonymized)
  - Trigger identification for each trait
  - Response strategies for each trait
  - Searchable database
  - User annotations: "This is what she does"

**Key Traits to Include:**
- Victim-playing / Playing the martyr
- Gaslighting
- Triangulation
- Love-bombing followed by devaluation
- Hoovering (pulling back in)
- Flying monkeys (using others to attack)
- Covert criticism disguised as concern
- Boundary violations
- Emotional manipulation
- Withholding affection/resources

#### 3.2 Manipulation Decoder
- **Purpose:** Real-time identification of manipulation tactics
- **Features:**
  - Text/message analyzer: Paste a message, identify tactics
  - Conversation replay: Analyze past interactions
  - Pattern matching: "This is similar to..."
  - Response suggestions (Grey Rock, boundaries)
  - Emotional impact assessment
  - Reality check: "Is this your fault?"

### 🎯 PRIORITY 4: CHILDREN-FOCUSED SUPPORT

**Research Alignment:** "Your emotional detachment is not selfish; it is the foundation of your children's future safety."

**Recommended Implementation:**

#### 4.1 Co-Parenting Stability Tracker
- **Purpose:** Monitor your emotional stability for children's benefit
- **Features:**
  - Daily stability check-in (1-10 scale)
  - Mood tracking linked to co-parenting interactions
  - "Sanctuary home" assessment: Is my home a safe space?
  - Children's emotional safety checklist
  - Parenting wins tracker
  - Stress management for co-parenting

#### 4.2 Children's Boundary Builder
- **Purpose:** Teach children healthy boundaries without alienating other parent
- **Features:**
  - Age-appropriate boundary education
  - Conversation starters for difficult topics
  - What to say when children ask about the other parent
  - Protecting children from manipulation
  - Recognizing signs of abuse in children
  - Resources for child therapy/support

---

### 🎯 PRIORITY 5: COMMUNITY & PEER SUPPORT

**Research Alignment:** Empaths need connection and validation from others on similar journeys

**Recommended Implementation:**

#### 5.1 Peer Support Groups (Enhanced)
- **Purpose:** Connect with others in similar situations
- **Features:**
  - Moderated discussion groups by topic
  - "Reality Anchor Routine" accountability groups
  - Weekly check-ins
  - Success story sharing
  - Peer mentorship matching
  - Anonymous participation option

#### 5.2 Accountability Partner System
- **Purpose:** Support consistency in recovery practices
- **Features:**
  - Find accountability partners
  - Daily/weekly check-in reminders
  - Shared progress tracking
  - Encouragement messages
  - Milestone celebrations

---

### 🎯 PRIORITY 6: ADVANCED ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS

**Research Alignment:** "This objectifies her behavior. It turns a confusing, painful interaction into a data point."

**Recommended Implementation:**

#### 6.1 Behavior Pattern Dashboard
- **Purpose:** Visualize manipulation cycles and your progress
- **Features:**
  - Timeline of incidents with NPD traits
  - Escalation pattern graphs
  - Trigger identification dashboard
  - Your response patterns (emotional vs. logical)
  - Progress metrics: Emotional detachment level
  - Comparison: "How I responded then vs. now"
  - Predictive alerts: "Based on patterns, watch for..."

#### 6.2 Recovery Progress Metrics
- **Purpose:** Quantify emotional healing
- **Features:**
  - Emotional stability trend
  - Boundary strength assessment
  - Empathy redirection progress
  - Expectation management score
  - Self-compassion growth
  - Children's stability correlation
  - Milestone achievements

## CONCLUSION

The current Reclaim app provides excellent foundational support for trauma survivors. By implementing these research-aligned features, the app can become a comprehensive recovery platform that specifically addresses the unique challenges of co-parenting with a narcissistic partner while maintaining emotional detachment and building a stable, healing environment for both the survivor and their children.

The **Reality Anchor Routine** should be the flagship feature, as it directly implements the research's core therapeutic practice and provides daily structure for emotional healing.

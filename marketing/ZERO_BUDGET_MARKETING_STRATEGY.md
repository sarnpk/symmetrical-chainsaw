## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Consistency > Perfection**: Post daily even if not perfect
2. **Engage First**: Spend 50% time engaging, 50% posting
3. **Repurpose Everything**: 1 video = TikTok + Instagram + YouTube
4. **Hook in 3 Seconds**: First 3 seconds determine if they watch
5. **CTA Every Post**: Always include "link in bio"
6. **Trending Audio**: Use trending sounds on TikTok
7. **Hashtag Strategy**: Mix popular + niche hashtags
8. **Post Times**: 7am, 12pm, 7pm (when people scroll)
9. **Batch Create**: Film 10 videos at once
10. **Analyze Weekly**: Double down on what works

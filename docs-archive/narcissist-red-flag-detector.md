# 30-Second Narcissist Red Flag Detector

## Quick Assessment Checklist
Rate each behavior (0 = Never, 1 = Sometimes, 2 = Often, 3 = Always)

### Love Bombing Phase
- [ ] Excessive compliments early in relationship (0-3)
- [ ] Pushes for quick commitment/intimacy (0-3)
- [ ] Claims you're "soulmates" within weeks (0-3)
- [ ] Overwhelming gifts or attention (0-3)

### Control & Manipulation
- [ ] Monitors your activities/communications (0-3)
- [ ] Isolates you from friends/family (0-3)
- [ ] Makes decisions for you without asking (0-3)
- [ ] Uses guilt or threats to get their way (0-3)

### Lack of Empathy
- [ ] Dismisses your feelings or concerns (0-3)
- [ ] Shows no remorse for hurting you (0-3)
- [ ] Makes everything about themselves (0-3)
- [ ] Cannot handle criticism or feedback (0-3)

### Emotional Abuse Patterns
- [ ] Gives silent treatment as punishment (0-3)
- [ ] Humiliates you in public or private (0-3)
- [ ] Threatens to leave during arguments (0-3)
- [ ] Blames you for their bad behavior (0-3)

## Scoring
- **0-12**: Low risk - Stay aware of patterns
- **13-24**: Moderate risk - Consider setting boundaries
- **25-36**: High risk - Seek support and safety planning
- **37-48**: Severe risk - Contact domestic violence resources immediately

## Red Flag Phrases to Watch For
- "No one will ever love you like I do"
- "You made me do this"
- "If you really loved me, you would..."
- "You're lucky to have me"
- "I'm the only one who understands you"

## Trust Your Instincts
If you're questioning whether someone's behavior is normal, it probably isn't. Healthy relationships don't require constant self-doubt.

## Next Steps
1. **Document patterns** - Keep a private journal
2. **Reach out** - Contact trusted friends or professionals
3. **Safety first** - If you feel unsafe, prioritize your wellbeing
4. **Professional help** - Consider therapy for objective guidance

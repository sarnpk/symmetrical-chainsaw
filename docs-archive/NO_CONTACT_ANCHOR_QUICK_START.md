# No Contact Anchor - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 What Is This?

A powerful tool to help you stay strong when you're tempted to contact your narcissistic abuser. Think of it as your "emergency brake" during withdrawal.

## 🎯 Core Features

### 1. **Why I Left** 
Document the abuse so you never forget why you left.

### 2. **Streak Counter**
Track days since no contact - visual motivation to keep going.

### 3. **Crisis Button**
Big red "Before You Contact Them" button that shows:
- Your documented abuse incidents
- Your current streak
- Safe people to call instead

### 4. **Urge Logging**
Track when you're tempted - see your progress as urges decrease.

### 5. **Safe Contacts**
Quick access to therapist, friends, family - call them instead.

## 📋 Setup (5 minutes)

1. **Navigate to** `/no-contact-anchor`

2. **Add "Why I Left" entries**
   - Click "Add Why I Left"
   - Describe the abuse incident
   - Tag the type (gaslighting, rage, etc.)
   - Note the emotional impact

3. **Add Safe Contacts**
   - Click "Add Safe Contact"
   - Add therapist, friends, family
   - Include phone numbers

4. **Set Start Date**
   - When you log your first urge, set your no-contact start date

## 🆘 Crisis Mode

**When tempted to contact them:**

1. Click the big red **"Before You Contact Them"** button
2. Read your documented abuse incidents
3. See your streak (don't break it!)
4. Call a safe contact instead
5. Log the urge (you resisted! 💪)

## 📊 Track Progress

- **Streak Counter**: Days since no contact
- **Urge Logs**: See how urges decrease over time
- **Stats**: Total urges logged, average intensity

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Add entries immediately** - Document abuse while it's fresh
2. **Be specific** - "He screamed at me for 2 hours" not "He was mean"
3. **Update safe contacts** - Keep phone numbers current
4. **Log every urge** - Track your progress
5. **Use crisis button** - Don't trust your memory in crisis

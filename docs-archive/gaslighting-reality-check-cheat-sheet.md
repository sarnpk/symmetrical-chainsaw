# Gaslighting Reality Check Cheat Sheet

## What is Gaslighting?
Gaslighting is a form of psychological manipulation where someone makes you question your own reality, memory, or perceptions.

## Common Gaslighting Phrases
- "That never happened"
- "You're being too sensitive"
- "You're imagining things"
- "I never said that"
- "You're crazy/overreacting"
- "Everyone thinks you're..."
- "You have a bad memory"

## Reality Check Questions
Ask yourself:
1. Do I feel confused after conversations with this person?
2. Am I constantly second-guessing myself?
3. Do I apologize frequently for things that aren't my fault?
4. Do I feel like I'm "walking on eggshells"?
5. Have others noticed changes in my confidence?

## Trust Your Gut Indicators
✅ Your feelings are valid
✅ Your memories are real
✅ You have the right to your emotions
✅ Confusion after interactions is a red flag
✅ You don't need to justify your reality

## Immediate Action Steps
1. **Document incidents** - Write down what happened immediately
2. **Seek outside perspective** - Talk to trusted friends/family
3. **Trust your instincts** - If something feels wrong, it probably is
4. **Set boundaries** - You don't have to engage in circular arguments
5. **Consider professional help** - Therapists can provide objective support
